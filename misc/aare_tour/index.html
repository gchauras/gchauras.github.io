Aare river bike tour

Bike tour along the Aare river starting from its mouth, where Aare drains into Rhine on the Swiss-German border, to its source on Grimselpass. I slept in hotels and enjoyed burgers along the way. This tour is marked as Aare route 8 and routes through quiter streets (when possible) and gravel (~20%). I chose to do this tour in reverse to enjoy some climbing and combined some stages to make it a little more challenging.

DAY 01

Koblenz → Aarau → Solothurn

Quiet riding along rural roads and gravel. Bird watching spots and a pass by Gösgen power plant.

DAY 02

Solothurn → Bern

Some amazing gravel riding along Nidau Kanal and asphalt riding along Hageneck canal.

DAY 03

Bern → Thun → Meiringen

Lakeside riding with moderate traffic and the perfect blue waters that make this region so photogenic. Waited at a level crossing for a fighter jet to taxi by in Meirigen.

DAY 04

Meiringen → Grimselpass (2178 m) → Oberwald

Climbing day. Unbelievable views of cliffs and dams on the ascent as the Aare rivier now looks no bigger than a stream gushing down.
